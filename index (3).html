Multiplayer

Play with friends on the same map. Everyone opens this page, then uses the same room code and map. The first player in becomes the host, so they need to stay in the game.

Missions

Drive: W/S or ↑/↓ for throttle and reverse, A/D or ←/→ to steer. On a phone use the on-screen buttons.

Earn: you only make money while the boat is actually moving. The faster you go, the more you earn.

Camera: press C or tap View to switch between third and first person. In first person you sit on the left of the back seat, angled slightly toward the motor; move the mouse to look around (or hold Q or E to look over your shoulder).

Wheelies: hold Shift (or UP on a phone) while on the throttle to pop the bow up. Bigger motors wheelie much higher.

Islands: drive up one and you’ll slide straight off (no engine on dry land). Bushes bounce you back.

Jet skis: buy one in the Boats tab. They corner much harder than a boat and lean right over. In the Upgrades tab you can buy adjustable trim: hold X to trim the bow up (faster flat out, loose in corners) or Z to trim it down (fast launch, sharp turns).

Waterskiing: on the lake screen pick Waterski. An AI driver tows you. Press W to start, then W / S ask the driver to go faster or slower, A / D carve across the wake, and cross the foam to jump. Press SPACE in the air for a spin trick, but land it or you wipe out. Skiing outside the wake pays more.

Minimap: the round map on the right shows the lake, you (red arrow) and every other player in multiplayer. Tap it to zoom.
